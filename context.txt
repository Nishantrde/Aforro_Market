8. Design decisions and why
• Stock lives per store (StoreProduct), and a cart creates one order per store. Never convert the old Store.products many-to-many in place; backfill with a script.
• Validate first, write inside atomic(), and lock stock rows with select_for_update() so two buyers cannot take the last item.
• Price snapshot on OrderItem so old orders never change when prices change.
